About — Garyukai

Eight decades with the brush.

Founded in 1940 to advance the study and teaching of calligraphy, Garyukai publishes the monthly journal Garyu — now past its 1,850th issue, the longest-running publication of its kind in Japan. Across some 130 chapters nationwide, nearly 2,000 members continue to devote themselves to the art of the written word.

Philosophy — The Garyukai Spirit

In every character, a heart.
In every character, individuality.

Garyukai is a calligraphy society that honors the individuality of each person. Rather than measuring skill as merely good or poor, we cherish self-expression through the written character. Just as every person has a heart, every character carries the spirit of the one who wrote it. We nurture that individuality — and the quiet joy of the brush.

Introduction — The Master

Taku Higano

日賀野 琢

President, Garyukai Co., Ltd. · Third-generation head of Garyukai

Born in 1964 in Utsunomiya, Tochigi, Taku Higano was raised amid the scent of ink and took up the brush in early childhood. As the third-generation head of Garyukai, he leads the society while pursuing his own practice as an artist. Beyond his own work, he shares the depth and delight of calligraphy through lectures and demonstrations. His specialty is the ancient scripts — seal and clerical.

Member, NittenStanding Director, Yomiuri Shohokai Auditor, All-Japan Calligraphers FederationDeputy Secretary-General, Kenshin Shodokai Lecturer, Saitama Gakuen University

Nitten — Japan’s foremost national fine-arts exhibition. Yomiuri Shohokai — one of Japan’s principal calligraphy associations. His specialty is the ancient seal and clerical scripts.

Frequently Asked Questions

What kind of works can I commission?

From company logos and shop signage to commemorative pieces and gifts, we create works to your request — proposing the style and scale to suit your setting and budget.

How does the creation process work?

We clarify your intent through a hearing sheet and a dedicated coordinator, then present a pencil draft before the work is brushed, so the result matches your vision.

Can you host corporate training or events?
